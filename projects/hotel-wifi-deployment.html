Project Overview

In modern luxury hospitality, high-speed and seamless Wi-Fi is consistently rated as the single most critical amenity by business travelers and conference attendees. Sunland International Hotel required a complete redesign of its wireless network to overcome thick concrete structural walls and support hundreds of concurrent devices during major conferences.

I performed comprehensive RF heat-mapping and predictive propagation surveys using Ekahau, determining optimal ceiling mount access point placements. We deployed Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) dual-band access points powered by 802.3at PoE+ switches.

The network features dynamic RF transmit power auto-tuning, fast BSS transition roaming (802.11r/k) for uninterrupted video calls while walking through hallways, and a branded captive guest portal with room-number authentication.

Engineering Objectives

Zero Coverage Dead Zones
Maintain minimum -65 dBm signal strength in all 100+ guest suites, dining verandas, and underground elevators.
500+ Concurrent Capacity
High-density channel planning (non-overlapping 20/40MHz channels) for full conference hall assemblies.
Fair-Share Bandwidth Shaping
Dynamic token bucket traffic policies preventing single clients from saturating the hotel's ISP fiber pipe.
Guest Device Isolation
Client Layer-2 wireless isolation preventing guest devices from probing or communicating with each other.
