Project Overview

Electro Engineering & IT Services is a premier technology integrator in Addis Ababa offering structured cabling, CCTV surveillance, optical fiber splicing, and IT equipment supplies. The company required an authoritative digital presence and online store to showcase service offerings, publish hardware inventory, and streamline customer inquiries.

I engineered the complete full-stack web application with a responsive product catalog, dynamic price filtering, service appointment booking calendar, shopping cart state management, and administrative control panels for product inventory updates.

The platform includes automated PDF quotation generation, custom inquiry workflows for commercial tenders, and seamless mobile responsiveness ensuring rapid browsing across desktop and smartphone devices.

Business Goals & Outcomes

Hardware Catalog Showcase
Publish real-time pricing and technical datasheets for Cisco, Hikvision, Grandstream, and Ubiquiti hardware.
Online Service Booking
Enable clients to book site surveys, cabling audits, and CCTV maintenance visits directly through an interactive calendar.
Instant Proforma Invoices
Generate official branded proforma PDF invoices for corporate procurement departments in seconds.
SEO & Local Visibility
Rank on Google page 1 for IT cabling, CCTV installation, and network hardware in Addis Ababa.

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Architected the complete full-stack web application using PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS.
  • Implemented shopping cart, wish list, product comparison, and category filtering algorithms.
  • Built the admin inventory management dashboard with image upload resizing and stock low-level alerts.
  • Configured web hosting on Linux VPS with Nginx, SSL certificates, and automated weekly database backups.
