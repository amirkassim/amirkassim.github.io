Tosegn Pharmacy ERP & Centralized POS System

A mission-critical enterprise pharmaceutical management platform engineered with prescription safety checks, high-throughput barcode checkout, FEFO/FIFO batch expiry monitoring, supplier procurement workflows, and real-time multi-branch inventory synchronization.

Category Enterprise Software & Systems

Tosegn Pharmacy ERP — Central Management Dashboard

Project Overview

Tosegn Pharmacy operates multiple high-volume pharmaceutical dispensaries serving hundreds of outpatient customers daily. Prior to this implementation, inventory accounting, batch tracking, and retail billing were hindered by legacy manual registries and isolated ledger systems, resulting in checkout bottlenecks, inventory discrepancies, and expired stock write-offs.

I was contracted to engineer a custom, end-to-end Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform and Point of Sale (POS) solution tailored specifically to regulatory pharmaceutical compliance in Ethiopia. The system centralizes drug catalog management, batch tracking with First-Expired-First-Out (FEFO) dispensing rules, instant barcode scanning, prescription audit logs, and automated supplier re-ordering.

The architecture features an ultra-reliable client-server topology backed by a relational SQL database with automated encrypted off-site backups, role-based security access, thermal receipt printer integrations, and sub-second inventory lookups across branch locations.

Business Goals & Objectives

Zero Stock Wastage
Enforce FEFO (First-Expired-First-Out) logic to alert pharmacists 90, 60, and 30 days prior to drug expiration dates.
Rapid Checkout Speed
Sub-second barcode scan resolution and automatic price calculation to slash patient queue wait times by over 70%.
Regulatory Audit Readiness
Tamper-proof audit logs for controlled medication dispensing, prescription archivals, and daily tax reconciliation reports.
Automated Reordering
Trigger automated purchase orders to registered pharmaceutical distributors when stock hits minimum safety threshold.

Key Challenges & Technical Hurdles

Legacy Data Migration
Parsed and normalized over 12,000 legacy medicine SKUs, batch histories, and pricing matrices with zero data corruption.
Offline Resiliency & Sync
Implemented local queue caching so cashier terminals continue billing uninterrupted even during temporary network drops.

My Role & Responsibilities

As the Lead Systems Architect and Full-Stack Developer, I managed the entire development lifecycle from requirements gathering with head pharmacists and accountants to database modeling, UI implementation, hardware integration, user training, and production maintenance.

  • Engineered the normalized relational database schema with ACID-compliant transactions for high-concurrency billing.
  • Built the high-speed Point-of-Sale (POS) cashier interface supporting omnidirectional barcode scanners and thermal printers.
  • Designed the FEFO batch expiry algorithm with color-coded dashboard indicators and daily automated email digests.
  • Implemented Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) strictly separating Cashier, Pharmacist, Storekeeper, and Administrator permissions.
  • Conducted on-site staff training sessions and produced comprehensive user manuals and SOP documentation.

Key Features & Deliverables

Barcode POS Cashier Register
Instant 1D/2D barcode recognition, keyboard shortcuts for rapid checkout, multiple payment modes (Cash, Telebirr, CBE Birr), and instant thermal receipt printing.
Batch & Expiry Date Management
Automatic FEFO drug routing, quarantine batch flagging, early-warning expiry alerts, and supplier return tracking with automated credit note generation.
Multi-Branch Inventory Sync
Real-time stock ledger synchronization across branch dispensaries, inter-branch stock transfer orders, and unified central warehouse audits.
Financial & Tax Auditing
Daily Z-Reports, gross profit margin calculation per SKU, automated VAT tax segregation, and exportable financial reports (Excel, PDF, CSV).
