Multi-Floor CCTV IP Surveillance Network & Security Operations

A high-security, intelligent IP surveillance network spanning multi-story hotel buildings and commercial complexes, featuring 80+ Hikvision 4MP/4K cameras, dedicated Gigabit PoE distribution switches, 64-channel RAID-5 NVR storage arrays, smart line-crossing video analytics, and a multi-display security command center.

Project Overview

Modern enterprise facilities, hotels, and commercial establishments require uncompromising physical security, loss prevention, and compliance recording. For this enterprise deployment in Addis Ababa, the objective was to replace vulnerable legacy analog cameras with an ultra-reliable, high-definition IP-based surveillance ecosystem capable of 24/7 recording with intelligent perimeter analytics.

Holding official Hikvision Certified Security Associate (HCSA) certification, I led the survey, lens focal length calculations, network bandwidth dimensioning, camera installation, and NVR storage configuration. We deployed 80+ vandal-proof dome and bullet IP cameras across building perimeters, subterranean parking basements, elevator lobbies, cash-handling front desks, and guest hallways.

The cameras are segmented onto an isolated surveillance VLAN (VLAN 40) powered by Gigabit 802.3af/at PoE+ switches, eliminating network congestion on enterprise business networks. The footage is recorded redundantly on Hikvision 64-channel NVRs configured with enterprise Western Digital Purple drives in RAID-5 configuration, retaining over 60 days of continuous H.265+ compressed video.

Key Technical Challenges & Solutions

Extreme Lighting & Glare
Enabled 120dB True WDR (Wide Dynamic Range) on glass entrance cameras to clearly capture faces against bright Ethiopian sunlight.
Elevator Traveling Cable Interference
Engineered elevator shaft cameras using shielded Cat6 STP cabling and vibration-damped mounts to eliminate motor electromagnetic noise.

My Role & Responsibilities

As Lead CCTV Systems Engineer and Certified Hikvision Specialist (HCSA), I directed the complete hardware deployment, network configuration, and security officer training.

  • Drafted comprehensive CCTV zone layout maps, lens focal angle calculations (2.8mm, 4mm, 6mm), and lighting lux surveys.
  • Installed and focused 80+ Hikvision vandal-resistant dome cameras and motorized varifocal bullet cameras.
  • Configured 64-channel NVRs, initialized RAID-5 disk arrays with hot-spare drives, and configured motion detection schedules.
  • Configured isolated Layer-2 surveillance VLANs with port security and DHCP snooping on Cisco switches.
  • Built the multi-display video wall control room in iVMS-4200 and conducted operator training for security guards.
