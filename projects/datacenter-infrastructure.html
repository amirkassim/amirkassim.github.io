Project Overview

High-performance enterprise networking relies fundamentally on the quality, reliability, and precision of its physical layer. For Sunland International Hotel and accompanying commercial facilities, the challenge was to engineer a clean, scalable, and standardized cabling infrastructure that eliminates packet loss, reduces latency, and supports 10-Gigabit backbone throughput for the next 15 years.

I supervised and executed the complete physical plant engineering, establishing the Main Distribution Frame (MDF) server room and Intermediate Distribution Frames (IDFs) on every accommodation level. Over 500 Cat6A copper endpoints and OM3 10GbE multi-mode fiber optic backbone runs were terminated, labeled with ANSI/TIA-606-B standard nomenclature, and tested using Fluke CableAnalyzers.

The facility incorporates dual APC Smart-UPS online power units, automated environmental temperature/humidity sensors with SMS triggers, overhead wire mesh cable trays, and grounded copper equipotential bonding bars.

Key Engineering Objectives

10G Fiber Backbone
OM3 multimode fiber connecting MDF core switches to all floor IDFs with 10Gbps line rate capacity.
Fluke Certified Terminations
100% pass rate across all 500+ copper drops for NEXT, return loss, and wiremap attenuation standards.
Clean Aesthetic Cable Dressing
Color-coded patch leads with Velcro cable binding ensuring clear port visibility and easy troubleshooting.

Dual APC Smart-UPS battery backups providing 4 hours of emergency power runtime with generator auto-transfer.
